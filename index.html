THE BASICS

HOW TO PLAY

Mido is simple to learn, but mastering the board is another story.

01

PICK

Choose a hole on your side and pick up every stone inside it.

02

MOVE

Move one stone into each successive hole around your board.

03

CONCENTRATE

Build your stones together and look for the winning move.

04

WIN

Get all 48 stones into a single hole.

THE GAME

MORE THAN A GAME

Mido is a strategy game built around concentration, planning and patience. Every stone changes the board. Every move creates another possibility.

Two players. Thirty-two holes. Ninety-six starting stones. One winner.
